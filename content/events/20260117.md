Hi folks, Rust Delhi hosted another meetup on Saturday, January 17, 2026. It was a fantastic event that brought together Rust enthusiasts for talks, discussions, and networking.

The venue was graciously provided by our friends at [Aftershoot](https://aftershoot.com), at Nexus Towers, Saket!

### Event Agenda:

1. Introductions.
2. "Stop reprocessing everything: Incremental data pipelines with CocoIndex" by [Haleshot](https://github.com/haleshot).
3. "Oxidizing our beloved Delhi Metro" by [keogami](https://github.com/keogami).
4. "The fault in our traits" by [lunchspider](https://github.com/lunchspider).
5. Break.
6. "Exploring Stream Processing in Rust with RS2" by [Rajan Yadav](https://github.com/rajany080).
7. "A primer on terminal visualisation and practical profiling in Rust" by [draconacht](https://github.com/draconacht).
8. Session by Aftershoot.
9. Group Photo & Finish.

Hi folks, Rust Delhi hosted another Rust meetup on Saturday, May 17, 2025. It was a fantastic event that brought together Rust enthusiasts to share their experiences and discuss the future of the Rust Delhi community.

This meetup was co-hosted by [zylum.in](https://zylum.in)!

### Event Agenda:

This was yet another BOF (Birds of a Feather / Agendaless) meetup and We had talks and discussions on Rust and it's ecosystem.

Hi folks, Rust Delhi hosted meetup on Saturday, July 19, 2025. It was a fantastic event that brought together Rust enthusiasts to share their experiences and discuss the future of the Rust Delhi community.

The venue was graciously provided by our friends at [essentia.dev](https://essentia.dev)!

### Event Agenda:

1. Kickstarting Rust Delhi.
2. “Rust 101 and Poem crate for OpenAPI Compatibility” by Guinnes Singh.
3. "Setting up dev environment for Rust in linux kernel" by Shresth Prasad.
4. "POSIX Message Queues with Rust" by Tanveer Raza.
5. "Designing Microprocessors with Rust" by Sameer.
6. Networking + Food.
7. Product pitches by Aftershoot, YRAL and EMBETTER TECHNOLOGIES.
8. Hiring and Pitching. Open to anyone hiring for a Rust role, looking for a Rust role, or otherwise.
